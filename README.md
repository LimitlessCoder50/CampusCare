System Overview
The Web-Based Campus Asset Reporting and Ticketing System with SMS Integration for EVSU-OC web-based platform designed to make reporting damaged or malfunctioning campus facilities and equipment more convenient and organized. The system will allow students and other campus faculty members to report problems involving campus equipments and facilities such as computers, air conditioning units, electrical wirings,etc and other campus assets, without needing to report them personally to the maintenance teams office.

Users will be able to select a problem category and describe the issue and specify the location of the affected campus asset, and upload a photo as supporting evidence. Each submitted report will be recorded in the system and assigned a ticket to help the
user and maintenance personnel track its status and progress.
The system will also include SMS integration and category-based notification routing.

When a report is submitted, the system will identify the selected problem category and
send an SMS notification to the maintenance personnel assigned to handle that type of
concern. For example reports categorized as electrical problems will notify the personnel responsible for electrical maintenance.
Maintenance team can log in to review reports check the tickets assigned to them, check uploaded photos and update ticket status, by keeping everything in one place, the system encourage students and other EVSU-OC faculty members to report campus
assets problems, improve communication between users and maintenance personnel and support more organized handling of repair request within the EVSO-OC campus.

Problem Statement
In EVSU - Ormoc campus, problems involving campus assets such as facilities and equipments may remain unreported because some students do not know who to
approach, feel shy about reporting the issue personally, or find it inconvenient to report personally and also other faculties may not have the time or find it inconvenient to report
it directly to the maintenance office. As result the maintenance team may not become aware of these problems, causing for the equipments get delayed for repairment, reporting sufficient descriptions, locations
details or visual evidence may make it more difficult for maintenance personnel to understand the concerned. Identifying the appropriate personnel to handle each problem may also require additional communication when requests are not organized
according to their maintenance category.

To solve this problem the proposed system Web-Based Campus Asset Reporting andTicketing System with SMS Integration for EVSU-OC will provide an online web application where users can file a ticket, select problem category, describe the issue,
specify the asset location and upload a photo. Each report will be assigned a ticket for
monitoring its progress. The system will be integrated with SMS API service to send notification to the concerned maintenance personnel responsible for handling the problem based on the selected category.

The proposed system aims to make reporting more accessible, improve the routing of maintenance request provide clearer information about reported problems and support more efficient monitoring of campus assets concerns.
